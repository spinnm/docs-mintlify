In **Settings**, you manage tenant-wide defaults.

## Data protection & retention

You define how long call and dialog data is kept before it is deleted:

- **Retention of calls** (in days)
- **Retention of dialogs/transcripts** (in days)

Shorter periods mean stricter data minimization. Choose values that fit your legal and organizational requirements.

<Warning>
The **automatic, scheduled deletion** after the retention period expires is currently being finalized. The retention periods are already configurable; the planned automatic enforcement will follow.
</Warning>

## Appearance

Adjust the **primary color** of the interface to match your corporate design. It applies to all users of your tenant.

<Frame caption="Appearance: primary color with live preview.">
  <img src="/images/agenten/einstellungen-erscheinungsbild.png" alt="Appearance settings with primary color and preview" />
</Frame>

If the chosen color does not reach the minimum contrast of 4.5:1 everywhere in the light theme, the portal shows a notice. You can still save the color. Users who need more contrast then turn on contrast mode themselves.

## Display and language

Each user sets display and language **for themselves** in the top navigation bar. These are not tenant settings and apply only to you.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Display" icon="circle-half-stroke">
    Choose between **Light**, **Dark** and **Contrast mode**. Contrast mode uses a fixed color palette tested for accessibility and replaces the tenant's primary color.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Language" icon="language">
    The portal is available in **German** and **English**. Dates and times follow the selected language.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

As long as you don't actively select a display mode, the portal follows your operating system. If increased contrast is enabled there, it starts in contrast mode. Otherwise it follows the light or dark system setting.

<Note>
The language of the interface is independent of the **language of the agent** in the conversation – the latter is set per agent in the [model parameters](/en/agenten-verwalten).
</Note>
