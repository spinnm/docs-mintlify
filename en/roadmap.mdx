msg.Hello is continuously being expanded. This overview shows which features are currently being finalized (🚧) and what is on the roadmap (📋). It helps you gauge what you can already rely on today.

## In progress 🚧

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Standard connector runtime" icon="plug-circle-bolt">
    Execution is already live for connected systems (MCP servers, planorg PVS, demo connectors). The runtime for **self-implemented standard connectors** following the msg.Hello OpenAPI specification is currently being finalized. See [Integrations & Tools](/en/connectoren).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automatic data deletion" icon="trash-clock">
    Scheduled, automatic deletion of call and dialog data after the retention period expires. The periods are already configurable. See [Settings](/en/einstellungen).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Planned 📋

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Further direct integrations" icon="puzzle-piece">
    Preconfigured connectors for further established systems (e.g. Doctolib, ClickDoc) – with no implementation effort on your part. A [connector](/en/connectoren) for Principa (planorg) is already available.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Outbound calls" icon="phone-arrow-up-right">
    Agents that actively place calls (outbound), in addition to answering incoming calls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fine-grained agent permissions" icon="user-lock">
    Permissions that control access to individual agents.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Extended login federation" icon="key">
    Connecting multiple identity providers depending on the email domain.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
This roadmap is a snapshot and not a binding commitment regarding dates or scope. For the specific status and planning, please contact your msg representative.
</Info>
