This chapter guides you from your first login to an agent you can test.

## Prerequisite: your tenant

Your **tenant** (your organization's self-contained working environment) is **set up by msg**. As part of onboarding, msg also sets up your first **administrator account** and then hands the tenant over to you for independent management.

<Note>
So you do not have to create your tenant yourself. As soon as you have received your credentials, you can sign in and get started right away.
</Note>

## Signing in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the portal">
    Open the msg.Hello portal in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your account">
    Enter your email address. Sign-in is handled via single sign-on (SSO) – depending on your organization's configuration, through your usual corporate login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a tenant">
    If you belong to multiple tenants, select the one you want. Otherwise you land directly on the home page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your first agent

On the home page, an **onboarding checklist** guides you through the most important steps. The core is your first agent:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an agent">
    Create a new agent using the **agent wizard**. You assign a name and go through the basic settings. Details: [Manage agents](/en/agenten-verwalten).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a conversation flow">
    In the **flow editor**, define how the conversation should proceed – greeting, recognizing the request, transferring or hanging up. Details: [Build conversation flows](/en/flows-bauen).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a phone number">
    Assign the agent a phone number at which it should be reachable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    Check the agent with a **test call directly in the browser** before it goes live. Details: [Test an agent](/en/agenten-testen).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish the flow as a **version** and activate it. From then on, new calls use the active version.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Start small: an agent that greets, recognizes the request, and transfers to a human when needed is built in a few minutes and immediately useful. You then expand business automation via connectors step by step.
</Tip>
